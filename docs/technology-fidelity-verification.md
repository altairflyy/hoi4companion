# Technology fidelity verification

Compare reconstructed folders against real screenshots at known resolution and configuration. Record node membership, offsets, dimensions, backgrounds, connector routing, and unrepresented elements separately.

Historical measurements reported 319/326 reconstructed memberships and small geometry differences on sampled elements. These counts describe a particular test, not universal coverage. Rendering tests, screenshot comparison, and live HUD alignment provide different evidence and should not be reported interchangeably.

Detailed historical implementation notes and recorded evidence are preserved in the
[Italian reference](it/technology-fidelity-verification.md). Those records retain their original dates and
verification limits; they are not a claim that every check was rerun for this translation.
