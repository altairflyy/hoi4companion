# Events, answers, and HUD targets

Catalog events and options have explicit parent-child identity. The guide selects an event and an answer belonging to that event. Multiple-choice export filtering is based on scripted options; the actual visible choices may be fewer because of conditions.

HUD highlighting requires a qualified visible event/option and matching guide/campaign context. Event popups take priority over tree recommendations underneath them. Event disappearance, a click, or a closed panel does not prove which answer was executed. Static event chains represent possible script relationships, not a prediction of the campaign.

Detailed historical implementation notes and recorded evidence are preserved in the
[Italian reference](it/events-live.md). Those records retain their original dates and
verification limits; they are not a claim that every check was rerun for this translation.

## Author event answers in guides (2026-11-16)
Imported guides name event answers in shorthand. `native/guides/src/reference_candidates.rs` recognizes them for every checklist (in `runtime_definition::prepare`, and again after decomposition for new definitions) and binds a single answer through `political_binding`, so the HUD highlights it when the event popup is visible.
- Line shapes (`references`): "Mobilize The…", "- Recall Kocs…(25 CP)", "They will not… event (50PP)", "Which By God…: CW setup" (answer, then other work), "On Expand…done: We will acc…(15PP)" (timing lead-in, then the answer), several answers ("Absolutely Not… + There is a…", "A… / B…", "A…, B…"), and an answer written out in full as the whole line (3+ words, exact match).
- Matching (`AnswerNames`): the abbreviation is a prefix of the answer text, cut anywhere, including mid-word ("A New Bismarck…" = "A New Bismarckjugend…"); case, accents ("Osterr…" = "Österreichische…") and punctuation ("Which By God…" = "...which, by God, …") are ignored. The catalog's own matcher adds other kinds of entries for display.
- Choice (`choose`), in order: the author's "(NNPP)" note equals the answer's political power cost; the nation provably receives the event (`event_reach`); a single answer whose event namespace names the nation. Otherwise nothing is bound and the candidates are shown on the card.
- Several answers on one line: the first is bound; the others are listed in `reference_resolution.also` and shown on the card. Only the bound one is highlighted by the HUD. The step completes only when every answer is observed (`all`); see "Several actions" below for the cases left untracked.
- **Guide timeline** (2026-11-19, `event_timing`, `reference_candidates::timeline`): when several answers are still left, the line's planned day is the day the guide plans the next focus after it (the next focus step, or "On X…" trigger compiled to a focus, in the same track) to complete: the written costs of the guide's focuses up to that one, 7 days per unit, from the game start. An answer is dropped when its event certainly cannot fire by then: its earliest day is the cheapest traced caller chain (focus cost plus cheapest prerequisite chain; a caller event; a decision through the focuses its `allowed`/`visible`/`available` certainly require, AND only), divided by `FOCUS_SPEED_FACTOR` 1.5. No bound (answer kept) for an event that fires on its own (`mean_time_to_happen`, not `is_triggered_only`), that a script outside the traced shapes mentions (on_actions, history, scripted effects, joint focuses, `random_events`, `state_event`: catalog `callers_complete`), a flag or unknown caller, or a decision without a certain focus requirement, a mission (`days_mission_timeout`: its `timeout_effect` runs when `available` fails) or a decision a script activates (`activate_decision`, `activate_mission`, `activate_targeted_decision` skip its conditions). Focuses a script can complete (`complete_national_focus`, `unlock_national_focus`, `reduce_focus_completion_cost`, history blocks of every date included, since a guide may start at a later bookmark), bypassable or joint focuses, and costs that are not a written number count 0. The catalog stores the bound on each event row (`earliest_day`, computed in `catalog_generation` over every focus tree, eligible or not). On what is left the existing choice rules run again (`choose`, then `reached`; recorded as `then`), else a single compatible answer left that the nation provably receives is taken; it binds with basis `guide_timeline` and the record in `reference_resolution.timeline` (a plan, not a proof: the step still completes only by the live event observation); several left keep the narrowed list with `reference_candidates.timeline`.
- **Spellings**: "cannot", "can not" and "can't" are searched as the same word. When several answers are left and some match the guide's own words literally, the answers found only through another spelling are dropped (`spelling_only`; basis `literal_text` when one is then chosen, record `literal`).
- **Targeted decisions** (2026-11-19, `event_reach::target_tags`): a decision whose `target_trigger` holds `FROM = { tag = X }` or `FROM = { OR = { tag = X tag = Y } }` at its top level sends its `FROM = { country_event … }` events to those tags; any other target stays unknown. Decision timing already counts the focuses its `allowed`/`visible`/`available` certainly require, targeted decisions included.
- **Visible candidate** (2026-11-19, `hud_targets::visible_candidate`): an unbound line keeps its full list of possible answers (`reference_candidates.event_options`, at most 128). The HUD proposes one for highlight (`resolution: "visible_candidate"`, not a binding) only when the qualified popup reader shows exactly one of them, its text starts with the guide's words, the line is the current one of its track (every earlier non-reminder step done) and the next focus anchor is not done. The step's status still comes from the evaluation alone; a highlight never completes it.
- Neighbouring guide events were measured (IL-12) and not used: no candidate line had a bound neighbour with a certain causal order against a candidate (`event_timing::only_after`).
- Real catalog (50 installed guides, existing checklists): 36 answers recognized (was 16), bases: 6 cost, 17 received by the nation, 5 namespace. Regressions in `native/backend/tests/integration/real_catalog_contract.rs`; survey: `event_reference_survey` (opt-in `HOI4_REAL_CATALOG=1`).

## Abbreviated triggers, names and several actions (2026-11-18)
The same pass (`ground_references`) now also covers the other abbreviations authors leave on existing checklists. Nothing is guessed: a link is made only for one nation-compatible entity, otherwise the step keeps its candidates or a reason.
- **Triggers** (`trigger`, `ground_trigger`): "On X…: action", "On X…done: …", "On picking “X…”: …", "After/When/Around X…: …", and without a colon "On Coal… Develop Ersatz…" (the trigger ends at its first `…`, keeping a closing quote, "done" and a parenthetical alternative). X is resolved with the contract 3 rules (`entity_resolution::resolve` + `trigger_condition`) against the guide's grounded steps and its focus tree; focus, technology and decision triggers become the step's `when` (`focus.active/completed`, `research.*`, `decisions.active/executed`). Only a step with no `when` or with promote's unknown placeholder gets one. Equal best matches are settled only when exactly one tied candidate is a focus, technology or decision that the same guide names as a step (`entity_resolution::resolve_trigger`, recorded as `disambiguation: unique_guide_step` with the other tied entities as candidates: ITA "Strengthen…" -> Strengthen the Papacy). Still ambiguous (two tied guide steps, SOV "Expand…done"), unresolved, or with extra meaning ("On Reform The… & GER down", "On The National… (or when JAP war on CHI)") stays unknown in `trigger_resolution` with the reason and candidates.
- **Names** (`named_candidates`, `choose_named`, `bind_named`): decisions, advisors, technologies and ideas whose real name the abbreviation is a prefix of (same `normalize`: case, accents, punctuation, and game markup such as "£BoP_right_texticon" ignored). Another nation's tagged ID, an advisor of another nation's character file or a catalog-proven unavailable entry is dropped. Binding needs: no compatible event answer and no same-named focus of the guide's tree; the author's cost note not contradicted by the real cost; then either the note's cost picks exactly one (`cost_matches`), or a single candidate the catalog proves usable (`unique_name_for_nation`), or a single technology. Decisions now carry `political_power_cost` (numeric `cost`) and `command_power_cost` (the `add_command_power` the completion effect removes). Bound through `political_binding` (decision/advisor/idea live facts) or the research facts.
- **Several actions** on one line: each piece keeps its own note ("Liturgical…(70PP) + Convoke…(100PP)"), "(50PPx2)" applies to each of two pieces, a single trailing note after several pieces stays unassigned (it may be a sum). The first grounded piece is bound, the others are in `reference_resolution.also`, unresolved ones in `reference_resolution.unresolved`. The step's completion is `all` over every piece's completion, so one observed action never completes it. The line stays a manual reminder (recognized, `tracked: false`, `scope: "line"`) when a piece is unresolved, when the pieces are alternatives ("A… / B…", "A… or B…"), or when other work follows them ("Allow Volunteer…+ Intervene On… (25PPx2) & SPA war*").
- Decision triggers count the decision as done through `decisions.executed` or `decisions.history.executed`, like decision completions, so a "before" gate does not reopen once the recent-execution fact drops out.
- **Blocks** (multi-line containers of existing checklists): the lead line's trigger scopes the whole block (`when`); a name on the lead line is shown (`reference_resolution.tracked: false`) but never bound, since the block holds other work. New checklists split blocks into children first (contract 4), so each line is bound on its own.
- Real catalog, existing checklists, lines with `…` (`event_reference_survey`, families joined line by line with the earlier run): unbound 138 -> 110, candidates shown 27 -> 34. Triggers: single lines 24 of 43 now observable (4 already had a real `when`, 15 stay unknown), block leads 28 of 38. Names: 6 single lines bound plus 5 inside trigger lines; 8 block leads recognized. Hand check: every compiled trigger and every bound name was compared with the game's English names; no wrong link found. Still open: author typos ("Friz Todt…"), names absent from the game files ("Expand Lateran…", "Integrate Translei…"), ambiguous prefixes with several tied guide steps ("Expand…", "Bring…", "Establish…"; 2026-11-18: ambiguous 17 -> 9, compiled 52 -> 58), event answers matching several received events, and lines whose action is free text.

## Written-out actions on existing checklists (2026-11-18)
`reference_candidates::ground_action` reads the action of an existing single-line manual step (after its trigger: "On X…:", "ASAP:", "When …:"). Nothing is guessed: the whole action must parse and every target must resolve to one country (`resolve_country`; "UK", "US", "USSR" and "the …" are mapped to ENG, USA, SOV first).
- **Foreign actions** use the existing vocabulary (`foreign-tasks-v1.json`, `command_families::foreign`) plus one action on several countries ("REL UK, FRA, GER, USSR (40PP)", "REL AUS & HUN"). One target: bound through `political_binding::foreign`, completion kept as a campaign milestone. Several targets: completion `all` over every part, each part listed in `foreign_parts` and kept as its own milestone once observed while the step applies (`guide_observe::definitions`, `part: true`), so "REL A, B" completes once both were seen, even if one relation improvement ended. Until every part is kept, guide-core runs the live admission gate on the step (`foreign_part_tokens`): relations that already exist do not complete a step whose trigger is false or unknown, or that is skipped.
- **War**: new relation action `declare_war` ("war on X", "declare war on X", "guerra a X"), done when the player's outgoing `war:X` relation exists (`docs/foreign-interactions-live.md`). Extra words ("War on GRE, HUN (at the same time)", "War on ICE (refuse DEN surrender)") leave the line manual.
- **Not readable live**: justify a war goal, annex, integrate, invade, collaboration government. Recognized with their targets (`action_resolution.status: "not_observable"`) and shown on the card as "confirm it yourself"; never tracked. Trailing notes are ignored only for this recognition.
- Real catalog (`written_action_survey`, existing checklists, single-line manual steps naming such an action): before 0 understood; now 5 lines bound (9 foreign actions: 2 REL lines with 4 + 2 targets, 1 war on ENG, 2 guarantees), 59 recognized as not readable live, 26 already bound by the importer, 86 not understood (several verbs on one line "Invade & annex", conditions "(if they refuse)", vague targets "all subjects", "Vichy", "Gibraltar", laws named "War Bonds"/"War Economy" caught by the survey filter). Hand check of every bound and recognized line against the guide text: no wrong target.
