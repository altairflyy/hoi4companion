# Technology rendering fidelity

Technology pages follow installed GUI folders/subsections rather than a generic list. Use native geometry, captions, backgrounds, sprites, orthogonal connectors, and game-specific layouts when available.

Respect section visibility and distinguish unmapped catalog entities from nodes on a real sheet. Guide research capacity follows available slots and current guide/runtime output. Preserve nation-independent behavior and avoid a separate rules evaluator in the renderer.

Detailed historical implementation notes and recorded evidence are preserved in the
[Italian reference](it/technology-fidelity.md). Those records retain their original dates and
verification limits; they are not a claim that every check was rerun for this translation.
