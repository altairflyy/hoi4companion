# Special projects and breakthrough points

Project identity, compatible facility/specialization, active research, final completion, and breakthrough resources are separate observations. Starting a project can satisfy an explicit start dependency without completing its research.

The catalog derives only qualified literal unconditional breakthrough costs. The reader publishes typed specialization balances; partial or inconsistent resource data stays unknown without fabricating a zero. The HUD withholds an unstarted project when required points are insufficient or unknown. Facility compatibility and visible exact project identity must also agree.

Historical real-game evidence verified a zero-point balance, a land-project cost, and selected campaign data. Positive highlighting with sufficient points and all facility navigation combinations still require matching real-screen evidence; fixture success is reported separately.

Detailed historical implementation notes and recorded evidence are preserved in the
[Italian reference](it/special-projects-live.md). Those records retain their original dates and
verification limits; they are not a claim that every check was rerun for this translation.
