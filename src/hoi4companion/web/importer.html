01 · GIVE THIS TO YOUR AI

Complete guide creation prompt

Open a conversation with your preferred AI, paste this prompt and add the YouTube/web link or attachments. Ask it to return the file specified by the prompt. You can start immediately without acquiring sources in the application.

Read the prompt

AI files retain IDs, English names, relationships and only distinct aliases supported by English game references. Translations and unverified aliases remain inside the application.

Sections do not overlap: focuses; research; production and equipment; construction and states; special projects; politics, characters and decisions; events and choices. Attach only sections needed for the current analysis phase. The selected tree filters focuses and, when its country is known, excludes events, choices, laws, ideas, advisers and decisions with incompatible country conditions. For politics and decisions, the selected country is also assumed to be the original country; category and appointment restrictions are respected. Shared and uncertain entries remain included: this is not a complete prediction of the campaign. Names and ID prefixes do not prove country scope. Other sections remain global. Events and choices remain linked through event_id. Disable the event filter to include single-choice events. Without a catalog, uncertain IDs remain unresolved.

Full catalog, as an alternative to sections

Contains every category, including single-choice events. Do not attach it together with sections: this duplicates the same entries.

Prepare a video to attach to your AI

Original resolution up to 1080p · 15 FPS · H.265 · mono audio at 64 kbps. GPU/CPU comparison uses actual samples: hardware is preferred when its file is within 25% of the smallest result; otherwise x265 CRF 22 is used. Compatible masters are reused without re-encoding. Five-minute parts overlap by 10 seconds; the last part may be shorter. Each file stays within 150 MB: parts are shortened when needed. Hardware quality 22 is not exactly equivalent to CRF 22. The source is retained for frame extraction; earlier preparations of the same video are cleaned up after the new preparation succeeds.
